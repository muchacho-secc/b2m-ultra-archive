# Athlete Physiological Profile

*Source: 1,086 days WHOOP data (July 2023 - May 2026)*

## Physiological Baseline

- **RHR:** 48.8 bpm average — strong aerobic base
- **HRV:** 0.128 RMSSD baseline, improved +10.4% over 3 years
- **HRV range:** 0.011–0.232 (high volatility under stress)
- **Sleep:** 8.02 hrs/night average — not a limiting factor
- **Max recorded strain:** 20.72

## Recovery Profile

- Overall average: 54.7% (41.5% green, 34.9% yellow, 23.6% red)
- Recovers well after planned hard efforts — post high-strain next-day recovery (58.9%) better than overall average
- **Strain score is a more reliable coaching signal than recovery score** — lifestyle variables (alcohol, travel, social) significantly impact recovery without reflecting training capacity
- Easy days are the primary recovery risk, not hard days — confirmed independently by Strava (97% easy pace non-compliance rate)
