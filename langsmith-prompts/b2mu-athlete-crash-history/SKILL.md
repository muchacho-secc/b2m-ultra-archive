# Athlete Crash Pattern History
*Source: 1,086 days WHOOP data (July 2023 - May 2026)*

## Core Pattern
Athlete has strong capacity but a recurring boom-bust cycle:
build hard for 2-3 months → ignore declining recovery → crash 
for 2-6 weeks → recover → repeat. The mechanism is 
lifestyle-driven (football x3 + daily gym), not training error.
Primary coaching role is early warning, not motivation.

## Consecutive High Strain Intolerance
- 44 episodes of 3+ consecutive days above strain 14 identified
- 4 out of 5 longest streaks resulted in poor recovery (<50%)
- After 2 consecutive hard days, a recovery day is required

## Five Major Crash Periods

| Period | Duration | Avg Recovery | Cause |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| May 14 – Jun 27, 2024 | 44 days | 37.1% | Peak block + early ramp up |
| Nov 29 – Dec 13, 2023 | 15 days | 37.8% | Sustained high load |
| Jul 23 – Aug 10, 2025 | 18 days | 38.0% | UK trip |
| Dec 20, 2023 – Jan 1, 2024 | 12 days | 38.8% | Holiday period |
| Feb 4–17, 2026 | 14 days | 39.8% | Playing through toe injury |

**May 2024 case study — the critical mistake:**
- May 6–12: Recovery rebounded naturally to 66.6%
- Athlete immediately ramped strain back up to 14.95
- Collapsed to 38.0% by May 20–26
- Then raced Jun 4 (19.49 strain) during crash → avg 17% 
  recovery for 3 days following
- **Lesson:** Temporary rebound is not full recovery. Recovery 
  windows must be extended 2–3 weeks, not cut short.

## Race Recovery Data

| Pre-Race Recovery | Outcome |
|------------------|---------|
| ≥70% | Best outcomes — avg 70–85% post-race |
| 50–70% | Moderate — avg 55–65% post-race |
| <50% | High crash risk |
| <20% | Disaster |

**Sep 17, 2023 — worst case example (20.72 strain, started on 8%):**
- Day +1: 53% (seemed okay)
- Day +3: 30% (crashed)
- Day +5: 9% (second crash)
- Day +8: 83% (finally recovered)
- Cost: 8 days to return to green

**Rule:** Never race when 7-day rolling average recovery 
is below 60%.

## Key Risk Periods for B2MU 2026

- **August UK trip (Aug 1–17):** historically disrupts recovery 
  (Jul–Aug 2025 crash). Plan conservatively around this.
- **Post-football finish (Aug 22):** transition period, body 
  adjusting to load change — monitor closely
- **September:** must arrive at Sydney Half (Sept 13) fresh — 
  no crash windows acceptable in the 3 weeks prior
- **October:** protect the final 3 weeks before B2M (Oct 24) 
  at all costs
