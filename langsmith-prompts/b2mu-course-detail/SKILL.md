## COURSE OVERVIEW

**Distance:** 79.2km (AllTrails GPX confirmed) **Total elevation gain:** 1,648m (official race figure) **Max elevation:** 92m above sea level (North Head, km 75) **Start:** Bondi Pavilion (sea level) **Finish:** Manly Beach (sea level) **Surface:** Mixed — road, concrete promenade, sandstone trail, grass, beach, stairs, bush track

**The defining character:** Never high — never flat. Max 92m. But 1,648m of gain from constant repetition of short, sharp climbs and descents. Every flat section is temporary. The accumulation is what breaks people, not any individual climb.

## LEG 1 — BONDI → ROSE BAY (19.94km)

### The Cliff Section (km 0-10) — 630m elevation gain

**km 0-1.7: Bondi Beach flat**

- Bondi Pavilion start, promenade along beach
- Flat and fast — crowd energy is high
- DANGER ZONE: easy to run too fast here
- Crowd excitement + flat terrain + fresh legs = blow-up risk
- Target: RPE 4-5, hold back deliberately

**km 1.7-3.9: First major cliff climb — 78m peak**

- Mackenzies Point ascent begins at km 1.7
- Climbs rapidly to 78m — 43m of gain in under 300m
- This is the first real wake-up call of the race
- Walk it. Every runner who has done B2M walks this.
- Spectacular views — take 5 seconds to enjoy, then focus

**km 3.8-4.0: Coogee coastal ridge (\~76m)**

- Second significant peak — similar character to first
- Short descent then immediate re-climb
- Sandstone paths — technical footwork required
- Watch for loose gravel on descents

**km 5.3-6.4: Dover Heights cliff section — 71m peak**

- Third major climb cluster in first 10km
- Multiple short sharp spikes between km 5.3-6.4
- Most technical terrain on Leg 1
- Stairs appear here — uneven sandstone steps
- Walk all stairs even if feeling fresh

**km 6.4-10: Descent toward Watson's Bay**

- Long gradual descent — relief after the climbs
- Tempting to run fast — resist it, save legs for later
- Coastal path narrows in places — single file with walkers
- Public foot traffic begins here — pass politely

**km 10-19.94: Rose Bay flat section**

- Significant flat relief — 101m gain across 5km
- Promenade and road sections — faster pace possible
- Temptation to push here — do not
- This is where conserved energy from km 0-10 pays off
- Lyne Park (Rose Bay) = Leg 1 checkpoint
- Aid station here — stop and take nutrition every time

### Leg 1 Key Coaching Rules

```
Walk: all stairs, all climbs over 20m, first major descent
Run: flat promenade sections at RPE 5-6 maximum
Eat: first gel at km 10 whether hungry or not
Landmark: Watsons Bay — if you feel great here, you went too fast
Target time for sub-9: arrive Rose Bay by 2:30-2:45hrs
```

## LEG 2 — ROSE BAY → MILSON PARK (20.81km)

### The Harbour Section (km 20-40) — most runnable leg

**km 20-25: Rose Bay to Double Bay**

- Moderate rolling terrain 155m gain
- Mixed road and path — urban feel
- Nielsen Park: potential beach crossing depending on route
- More exposed to sun — apply sunscreen at Leg 1 aid station

**km 25-30: Botanical Gardens approach**

- Relatively flat 132m gain — fastest section of race
- Road and concrete promenade
- Opera House comes into view — iconic moment
- Emotional lift from scenery — do NOT let it inflate pace
- This is where negative split runners make their move — ignore them

**km 30-35: Botanical Gardens and CBD**

- 193m gain — moderate climbs through gardens
- Crowd support picks up around CBD
- Harbour Bridge crossing — spectacular but focus required
- Narrow sections through gardens — single file

**km 35-40: Milsons Point approach**

- 148m gain — rolling toward North Sydney
- Road surfaces — feet taking significant impact by now
- Milson Park (North Sydney) = Leg 2 checkpoint
- Last aid station before significant trail begins

### Leg 2 Key Coaching Rules

```
Walk: all stairs, climbs over 30m
Run: most of this leg can be run — it's the most runnable section
Eat: every 30-40 minutes — never skip an aid station
Landmark: How do you feel at the Harbour Bridge? 
  "Good" = you're on track for sub-9
  "Worked" = you went too fast on Leg 1 — recalibrate now
Target time for sub-9: arrive Milson Park by 5:00-5:15hrs
```

## LEG 3 — MILSON PARK → CLONTARF (23.24km)

### The Trail Section (km 40-63) — where races are won and lost

**km 40-50: Neutral Bay to Mosman**

- 345m gain across this 10km section
- Character changes dramatically — urban path to trail
- Narrow sandstone paths, technical footing
- Public foot traffic thins out — more space
- Mental shift required: this is where ultra mode begins

**km 50.4-51.2: Bradleys Head major climb — 67m peak**

- Most significant climb between legs 1 and 4
- Climbs 62m in under 800m — steep and sustained
- With 50km in legs this hurts more than it should
- Walk the entire climb — no exceptions
- Aid station near Bradleys Head — take everything offered

**km 51-55: Mosman descent and rolling**

- 234m gain in km 50-55 section — hardest km 50-55 section
- Multiple short spikes — uneven rhythm
- "Clontarf to North Harbour reserve is tough — the uneven stair heights mean you can't get into a rhythm" — Craig Johnson
- Foot placement critical — concentration required
- Walk all technical stair descents

**km 60-63.5: Cremorne/Mosman cliff climb — 80m peak**

- Highest point outside of North Head: 80m at km 63
- 253m of gain in the km 60-65 section — brutal at this stage
- Three distinct spikes visible on elevation profile
- With 60km in legs this is where people start walking everything
- Walk it — you should be walking it anyway
- Descents from 80m: sandstone trail, technical, use hands on rocks

**km 63.5-65: Clontarf descent**

- Relief descent to sea level before North Head begins
- Clontarf Reserve = Leg 3 checkpoint
- Last major aid station before the finish
- 17.81km remaining — but the hardest climbing ahead

### Leg 3 Key Coaching Rules

```
Walk: all stairs without exception, all climbs over 15m
     (fatigue means what felt manageable at km 5 costs 3x at km 55)
Run: flat and gentle sections only
Eat: every 30 minutes — gut gets harder to manage here
     switch to real food if available (fruit, rice, sandwich)
Mental: expect it to feel harder than predicted — that's normal
Landmark: Clontarf arrival is a psychological checkpoint
  You're at 63km with 17 to go. North Head awaits.
  You've trained North Head. This is where that pays off.
Target time for sub-9: arrive Clontarf by 7:00-7:15hrs
```

## LEG 4 — CLONTARF → MANLY (17.81km)

### North Head (km 65-79) — the race within the race

**km 65-70: Clontarf flat section**

- Brief flat relief — 137m gain across 5km
- Psychologically dangerous section — you can see Manly Wharf
- DO NOT speed up — North Head is coming
- This section feels easy after Leg 3 — that's a trap

**km 70.3-71.6: North Head approach — 75m sustained climb**

- Begins suddenly at km 70.3 — from sea level to 75m in 1.3km
- Sustained climb unlike the short spikes of earlier legs
- With 70km in legs this is a genuine physical challenge
- Power hike the entire climb — hands on knees
- 295m of gain in the km 70-75 section — hardest final section

**km 71.6-73.8: North Head plateau**

- Brief plateau before the final summit push
- Exposed to coastal wind and afternoon sun
- Mental resilience required — the finish feels close but isn't

**km 73.8: The Manly Wharf Moment** ⚠️ CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL LANDMARK ⚠️ At km 73.8 you see Manly Beach and the finish line — approximately 200m away across the water. Then the course routes you AWAY from the finish and UP to North Head's final summit.

Unprepared runners frequently break mentally here. Prepared runners know it's coming and have rehearsed it. This is why training Leg 4 on tired legs is mandatory.

**km 73.8-75.2: Final North Head summit — 92m (highest point)**

- The highest point of the entire course
- From 0m at Manly Wharf visible → 92m at summit
- Multiple switchbacks on sandstone trail
- Hands on rocks on steeper sections
- Walk every step of this climb — no heroics

**km 75.2-79.2: Summit descent to Manly**

- Long descent from 92m back to sea level
- 4km of technical sandstone descent on exhausted legs
- Quads are destroyed — short stride, upright posture
- Lean forward slightly — don't lean back (creates braking and hammers quads)
- Manly Beach finish line — "The finish line is totally worth it" — Craig Johnson

### Leg 4 Key Coaching Rules

```
Walk: everything steep — no exceptions
Run: flat sections only, Manly descent at controlled pace
Eat: keep fuelling even if nausea — stopping = bonk risk
Mental: "I've run North Head with tired legs in training.
         I know exactly what this feels like. Keep moving."
Landmark: See the finish then route away — this is known.
          It's not a mistake. It's the plan. Keep going.
Target time for sub-9: arrive Manly by 9:00hrs
```

Each aid station provides: hydration, nutrition, and medical support.

**IMPORTANT — 4 aid stations not 3:** There is a fourth aid station at East Esplanade Manly (\~70km) — mid-Leg 4. This is critical — you get resupplied just before North Head.

**Fuelling strategy by aid station:**

```
Aid Station 1 (km 20): Eat, drink, apply sunscreen
                        Take everything available
Aid Station 2 (km 40): Halfway check — head torch drop-off
                        Real food recommended
Aid Station 3 (km 60): Last major stop before North Head
                        Fill water to maximum (800ml minimum)
                        Eat a full meal — 20km remaining
                        Apply sunscreen — afternoon heat ahead
Aid Station 4 (km 70): CRITICAL — North Head climb begins immediately after
                        Take every gel, every drink offered
                        Last aid before the finish line
```

## MANDATORY SAFETY GEAR

Source: Official B2M race website — STRICTLY ENFORCED

**Required items (solo runners):**

- Head torch *(must be carried until Aid Station 2 at \~40km)*
- Water capacity minimum 800ml
- Mobile phone
- Compression bandage
- Thermal blanket
- Rain jacket *(may be required depending on weather)*

**Enforcement rules:**

- Gear checked at any point during the race
- **10-minute time penalty per missing item**
- Gear check also at finish line
- Head torch can be dropped at Aid Station 2 and collected at finish *(must be clearly labelled with your name)*

**Coach must reference this:** Mandatory gear adds weight to pack for 40km minimum. Train with a race vest carrying equivalent weight from BUILD phase. A 10-minute penalty could end a sub-9 attempt — no gear risks.

**Recommended beyond mandatory:**

- Gels/nutrition for sections between aid stations
- Anti-chafe (9 hours of continuous movement)
- Blister kit (sandstone terrain is hard on feet)
- Spare socks (optional but valuable at Leg 2 checkpoint)

## CRITICAL RACE NOTES

### Crowd and Traffic

The course uses public walkways throughout. Expect:

- High foot traffic in CBD and Bondi/Manly sections
- Narrow single-file sections on cliff paths
- Supportive public — people offer snacks and cheer
- Pass politely: "Passing on your right thanks!"
- Congestion at popular spots (Opera House, Harbour Bridge)

### Navigation

- Course marking is comprehensive — Craig Johnson: "I didn't get lost once"
- Follow the B2M markers religiously on technical sections
- Phone with GPS backup recommended on Leg 3-4 trail sections
- If uncertain, stop and check — wrong turns add precious km

### Weather on Race Day

- Start: cold at 5am — warm layers for the bus and pre-race
- Legs 1-2: cool morning, manageable
- Leg 3: warming up — sun exposure increases
- Leg 4: afternoon — hottest part of the race on exposed North Head
- October Sydney: 18-26°C typical — be prepared for either end
- Apply sunscreen at Leg 1 and Leg 2 aid stations

### Foot Traffic Flow Guide

```
Km 0-5 (Bondi cliff):    Busy — public walkers + runners
Km 5-15 (Watsons Bay):   Moderate
Km 15-35 (harbour):      Busy — popular walking routes
Km 35-50 (CBD/bridge):   Very busy — tourist areas
Km 50-63 (Mosman trail): Quiet — technical trail
Km 63-70 (Clontarf):     Quiet
Km 70-79 (North Head):   Quiet — most technical section
```

## RECCE PRIORITY GUIDE

Based on course difficulty and preparation impact:

| Priority | Leg | Why | Best Time to Recce |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | Leg 4 (North Head) | Psychological familiarity critical | PEAK phase |
| 2nd | Leg 1 (Bondi cliff) | Pacing discipline — most blow-ups | BASE/BUILD |
| 3rd | Leg 3 (Mosman trail) | Most technical — unfamiliar terrain | BUILD phase |
| 4th | Leg 2 (Harbour) | Most runnable — lower priority | Any time |

**Minimum requirement:** Every leg recce'd at least once before race day. **Ideal:** Legs 3 and 4 recce'd twice — once fresh, once on tired legs. **Non-negotiable:** North Head (km 70-79) must be trained with fatigued legs. Running it fresh does not prepare you for running it at km 70.
